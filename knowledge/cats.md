# Cat care

General guidance for planning a cat's day. Not veterinary advice: an owner's vet
always overrides these notes.

## Feeding
tags: feeding, food, meals, kitten, adult, senior, wet food
Adult cats are commonly fed two or more small meals a day at consistent times.
Kittens need more frequent meals, often three or four a day. A meal takes about
5-10 minutes. Fresh water should always be available; refreshing the bowl can be
done alongside a meal.

## Litter box
tags: litter, box, cleaning, hygiene, scoop
Scoop the litter box at least once a day (about 5 minutes) and do a full litter change
about weekly (about 15 minutes). Homes with more than one cat need more frequent
scooping. Litter box care is usually medium priority: a dirty box can lead to a cat
avoiding it.

## Play and enrichment
tags: play, enrichment, exercise, indoor, hunting, boredom
Indoor cats benefit from one or two short interactive play sessions a day of about
10-15 minutes, for example with a wand toy. Play before a meal mimics the natural
hunt-eat pattern. Enrichment is usually medium priority and can fill gaps in the day.

## Grooming
tags: grooming, brushing, coat, hairball, nails
Long-haired cats benefit from brushing daily or every other day (5-10 minutes) to
prevent mats and hairballs. Short-haired cats usually need brushing about weekly.
Nail trims are typically every 2-3 weeks. Grooming is usually low priority.

## Senior cats
tags: senior, older, age, kidney, check-up
Senior cats (roughly 11 years and older) may need smaller, more frequent meals,
easier access to the litter box and more frequent vet check-ups. Watch for changes in
appetite, drinking and litter habits and report them to a vet.
