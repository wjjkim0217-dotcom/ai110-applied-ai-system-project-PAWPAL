# Medication and health tasks

General guidance on scheduling medication and health care. Not veterinary advice:
always follow the dose, timing and instructions the vet gave.

## Medication timing
tags: meds, medication, pills, dose, fixed time, schedule
Medication should be given at the time the vet prescribed, so it belongs at a fixed
time with high priority. Twice-daily medication is usually given about 12 hours
apart (for example 08:00 and 20:00). Once-daily medication should be given at the
same time every day. Giving a pill usually takes about 5 minutes.

## Medication with food
tags: meds, medication, food, meal, stomach
Some medications must be given with food and others on an empty stomach. If the
owner says a medication goes with a meal, schedule it at or right after that meal
time. If the instructions are unknown, keep the time the owner gave and suggest
they check with their vet.

## Missed doses
tags: missed, dose, late, forgot
If a dose is missed, do not double up on the next dose unless a vet says so. The
plan should never schedule two doses of the same medication close together.

## Preventives
tags: flea, tick, heartworm, preventive, monthly
Flea, tick and heartworm preventives are typically given monthly. Schedule them as
a once task on the due date, high priority, about 5 minutes.

## When to call a vet
tags: emergency, vet, symptoms, sick, vomiting, lethargy
Planning tools cannot judge medical symptoms. If the owner mentions vomiting,
not eating, breathing trouble, collapse, or any sudden change in behaviour, the
assistant should tell them to contact a vet rather than schedule around it.
